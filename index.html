About Us

Root Flower is a beloved floral studio based in Kuching, Sarawak
dedicated to creating beautiful floral arrangements and focus on high quality for every occasion.

From heartfelt bouquets to decoration florals, they transform each stem into meaningful expression, whether it's love, sympathy, celebration, or a simple gesture of beauty.

What We Offer:

  • 🌷Signature Bouquets
  • 🌷Creative Designs including snack bouquets and themed arrangements for holidays and special events.
  • 🌷Custom Creations: Whether it's for weddings, celebrations, or solemn moments, Root Flower tailors each design to your needs.
  • 🌷Convenient Contact: Easily reach the team via WhatsApp at +6014-399 9709 or Instagram to request catalogues or book orders.

Our Approach

We blend artistic floral design with local cultural sentiment, crafting arrangements that resonate emotionally.
Our seasonal and festive collections, like our Qixi Festival bouquets, reflect our passion for bringing timely joy to our customers.
